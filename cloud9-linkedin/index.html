Overview

Cloud 9 Window Cleaning was created with one simple mission: to bring Sydney homeowners a cleaner, clearer and more premium window cleaning experience. What started as a small operation has grown into one of Sydney's highest-rated window cleaning teams. We're rated 5.0 stars on Google and trusted across the North Shore, Northern Beaches, Sutherland Shire, Eastern Suburbs, Inner West, St George and Greater Sydney. What we do • Residential window cleaning with our 4-in-1 deep clean: glass, frames, tracks and sills • Pure deionised water cleaning, a 100% chemical-free process • Solar panel cleaning • Glass balustrade and pool fence cleaning • Hard water spot removal Maintenance plans Quarterly, triannual and biannual plans keep your glass looking fresh all year. Every plan includes free Cloud 9 Rainblock Tech, and quarterly clients get our 365 Day Unlimited Touch Up Policy. How we work Every home we service gets the same care, attention and professionalism we'd expect for our own.
