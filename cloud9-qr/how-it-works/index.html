Customers scan one QR code and land on a simple Cloud 9 page. From there it is one tap to follow you, and one tap to leave a 5-star review.

How it works

A QR code can only open one link, so it opens one small page with three buttons. Each button opens the right app straight away.

Good to know

  • Follow is one tap after the scan. Facebook and Instagram don't allow a working Follow button inside another website, so each button opens the Cloud 9 page in the app where the Follow button is.
  • Google review opens the stars right away. The customer only needs to be signed in to Google (most phones already are).
  • The QR never needs reprinting. If a link changes, we update the page, and every printed QR keeps working.
  • Before printing, the page can move on your own website (for example cloud9windowcleaning.com.au/follow) so customers only ever see the Cloud 9 name.
