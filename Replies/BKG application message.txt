2) A test
Yes, I have tested pages before. On a client's enquiry page I changed one thing at a time (the headline, the button wording and the length of the request form) and compared the enquiries that came in before and after each change. What that taught me is that a small page can't give a clear A/B result: with only a few hundred visitors a month, a split test is mostly noise. So on a low-traffic page I make bigger changes one at a time (offer, headline, form length), compare against the baseline, and judge on leads and appointments, not clicks. I write down the hypothesis and the conclusion of every test, and I say clearly when the data is too small to decide.

3) The inspiration book funnel
On the page: one goal, a photo of the book, what is inside, a few real bathroom projects as proof and a short form (name, email, phone optional). In the form: one or two questions that show how serious the person is, such as when they plan to renovate and whether it is a full bathroom or a partial one. After the form: a thank-you page with the book download, a "book a showroom visit" button with the calendar, then an email a few days later with a project story and a free 3D design offer. Serious leads (renovating within 6 months) get a quick WhatsApp or call from sales, while early browsers get a slower email series. Because the purchase takes months, I would keep them warm instead of pushing for the appointment on day one.

4) Form to CRM, GA4 and Meta, with no lost leads
The form sends its UTM values in hidden fields and sends the entry to Bitrix24 through a webhook (via Make.com if needed), and the same submit triggers the confirmation email. The thank-you page fires the GA4 event and the Meta pixel conversion through Google Tag Manager, so a conversion only counts after a real submit. To check, I submit every route myself on mobile and desktop, then confirm the entry in WordPress, the lead in Bitrix24 with its source, the email in my inbox, and the events in GTM preview, GA4 DebugView and the Meta test events. After launch I compare form entries with CRM leads every week, so any gap shows up fast, and I set an alert for failed webhooks. Bitrix24, GTM and Gravity Forms are new for me, so I would build and test them on staging first.
