04 · Season plan

What we’ll post, and when

A simple plan for the year ahead, so there’s always a reason for clients to book.

November – December
Party season. Christmas party and New Year’s Eve hair, glam packages and the last Christmas appointments.
January
New year, new look. Fresh colour, new trends and treatments for after-Christmas hair, plus the team trip as fun behind-the-scenes content.
Late January – February
Valentine’s. Date-night blow-dries and colour, pushed from the last week of January.
March
Mother’s Day. Treat-your-mum ideas for hair and makeup.
April – September
Wedding season. Seventy Six Bridal every week, plus prom hair in May and June.
All year
Gaps every Monday, before & afters, client reviews and the girls behind the chair, because people buy from people.
