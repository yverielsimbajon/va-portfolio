My Valentine’s ideas

Valentine’s Day 2027 falls on a Sunday, when the salons are closed, so Saturday 13 February becomes the big day. It’s also Galentine’s Day, which gives us a lovely second angle for the girls. One idea for hair, one for makeup, both using your one-to-one lessons.

Hair

Love Your Hair

The offer
Date-night & Galentine’s blow-dries on Saturday 13 Feb, plus a “bring your bestie” option: two chairs side by side for you and a friend.
1:1 lesson
“Blow-dry like a pro”: a one-to-one lesson where our client learns to recreate soft, bouncy waves at home for date night. A lovely gift idea too.
Content
A reel of 3 date-night hairstyles from the team, a before & after of a Valentine’s blow-dry, and Stories with a “which look are you?” poll and a countdown sticker.
Push
Monday availability stories in February flagged as “Valentine’s week”, and every stylist shares the post on her own page.
Makeup

Date Night Glam with Kelsey

The offer
Valentine’s makeup for Saturday 13 Feb: soft glam for date night or a night out with the girls.
1:1 lesson
“Learn your date-night look”: Kelsey does one side of the face, our client copies it on the other, and leaves with a personal product list. Can also be gifted to someone you love.
Content
A makeup transformation reel, a carousel of “Kelsey’s 3 date-night tips”, and a Stories Q&A: “Ask Kelsey anything”.
Push
Pair it with the hair offer: “Hair & makeup, done before your date”, so one booking can include both.
