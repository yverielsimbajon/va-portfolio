04 · Valentine’s

Valentine’s at Seventy Six

Lots of ideas to pick from, for the salon, for the girls and for our clients. Valentine’s is about more than couples: date nights, Galentine’s with the besties, mums and daughters, and treating yourself. Pick your favourites and I’ll build the posts, reels and captions around them.

One-to-one lessons Sat 13 Feb

Idea 1 · Hair with Kimmy

Blow-dry like a pro

The idea
A one-to-one lesson on Saturday 13 February (Galentine’s Day): our client learns to recreate soft, bouncy waves at home before date night.
Gift it
“Give them a lesson they’ll love”: a lovely gift for partners, friends and mums to book for someone special.
The plan
  • When: Saturday 13 February, in one-to-one slots (you choose how many and how long).
  • Booking: through Goldie or a DM, first come first served.
  • On the day: Kimmy shows each step on one side, our client copies it on the other, then leaves with a short tips card.
  • Price: yours to set, and it can also be bought as a gift voucher.
Idea 2 · Makeup with Kelsey

Learn your date-night look

The idea
Kelsey does one side of the face, our client copies it on the other, and leaves with a personal product list.
Pair it
“Hair & makeup, done before your date”: one booking that includes both.
The plan
  • When: Saturday 13 February, one-to-one slots with Kelsey.
  • Bring: our client brings their own makeup bag, so Kelsey can show what to keep and what to add.
  • Takes home: a personal product list and the look on their own face for date night.
  • Add-on: book it with a blow-dry so hair and makeup are both done.

  • Treats: you choose them and set any limits.
Idea 6Early bird bookings

Book your Valentine’s appointment before Sat 6 Feb and get a free treatment added, so the diaries fill early.

The plan

Idea 7Valentine’s lucky draw

Clients enter through a simple form (name and email), like the post and share it to their Story. We spin an online wheel live on Friday 12 Feb. Prizes like a free colour, a treatment or a gift basket. Bonus: every entry grows our email list for future campaigns (with a tick-box so they agree to emails).

The plan
  • How to enter (25 Jan to Thu 11 Feb): fill in the form (name, email and a tick-box to agree to emails), like the lucky draw post, and share it to their Story tagging @seventysixsalon.
  • Who can win: anyone who did all three steps. One entry per person (the form checks for repeat emails).
  • Picking the winner: on Friday 12 February I put every valid entry into an online wheel and spin it, screen-recorded and shared on Stories so everyone sees it’s fair.
  • Checking: I make sure the winner liked the post and shared it. If not, we spin again.
  • Announcing: the winner is tagged on Stories and gets a DM. They have 48 hours to reply, or we spin again.
  • Prize: you choose (for example a free colour or a treatment), to be used by the end of March.
  • Small print: free to enter, UK only, 18+, and not run by Instagram. I write these rules on the post.
Idea 8Little love touches

A fresh rose, a mini chocolate and a handwritten thank-you card for every client that week, plus pink treats while they sit. Small, memorable, very shareable.

The plan
  • Who: every client from Monday 8 to Saturday 13 February.
  • Quantity: I count the bookings in Goldie for that week, so we know how many roses, chocolates and cards to buy.
  • Cards: the girls write a short thank-you with the client’s first name.
  • Budget: yours to set.

Getting the girls involved

Idea 13Galentine’s Glam Battle

Each stylist creates one Valentine’s look. We post them side by side, followers vote in a Story poll, and the winner gets a little prize from you. Fun for the team, and great reach as each girl shares it.

The plan
  • Create (w/c 1 Feb): each stylist does one Valentine’s look on a real client (with their OK to post) and sends me the photo by Friday 5 February.
  • Vote (Mon 8 to Thu 11 Feb, 8pm): I post all the looks numbered 1, 2, 3… Followers vote by commenting the number, or in a Story poll.
  • Who wins: the look with the most votes. I count the comments and poll results and screenshot them so it’s fair. If it’s a tie, Kimmy picks.
  • Announce: Friday 12 February on Stories, and the winning stylist gets a prize from you.
  • Bonus: each girl shares her look so her own followers vote too, which brings new people to the page.

Three creative extras

Idea 17Swipe right on your new look

A dating-app style carousel and Stories where shades have their own “profiles” (“Honey Blonde, loves sunsets and long walks to the salon”). Followers vote for their match, and the winning shade gets a special that week.

The plan
  • Post (w/c 1 Feb): a carousel and Stories with 4 shade “profiles” in your style.
  • Vote: followers vote in a Story poll (open 24 hours) or comment “swipe right” under their favourite shade.
  • Who wins: the shade with the most votes when the poll closes. I screenshot the results.
  • Prize: the winning shade gets a special during Love Your Hair Week, announced the next day.
Idea 18Blind date with a treatment

Wrapped, sealed envelopes at the desk. After the appointment, our client picks one to open: a free treatment, money off the next visit or a mini product. Lovely to film for Stories.

The plan
  • Envelopes: you choose what goes inside (a free treatment, money off the next visit, a mini product). Every envelope has a treat, so everyone wins something.
  • Who: every client during Love Your Hair Week picks one envelope after the appointment.
  • Fair: the envelopes are shuffled each morning, and we keep a quick list of what’s been given.
  • Content: clients who are happy to be filmed open theirs on camera for Stories.
Idea 19The love notes wall

A pink wall in the salon where clients write a love note to their bestie, their mum or themselves. We share the sweetest ones on Stories, and the favourite wins a treat.

The plan
  • Set up: the wall goes up on Monday 8 February, with pink cards and pens at the desk.
  • Entering: clients write their note, with their first name and Instagram name (or phone number) on the back so we can contact them.
  • Who wins: on Saturday 13 February the team picks their top 3 notes, then followers vote for the winner in a Story poll.
  • Prize: a treat on their next visit, contacted by DM or phone.
