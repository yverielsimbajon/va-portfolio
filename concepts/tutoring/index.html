FAQ

Questions parents ask

Is the first assessment really free?

Yes. It's a 45-minute session with no obligation. You'll get a short written summary of strengths, gaps and our recommendation.

Do you offer online tutoring?

Absolutely. Online sessions use a shared whiteboard and the same tutors and materials as our Chatswood centre.

What if my child doesn't click with their tutor?

Just let us know and we'll rematch them, free of charge. The right fit matters more than anything.

Can I pause or cancel?

There are no lock-in contracts. Pay per session or by the term, and pause any time with a week's notice.

Are your tutors checked?

Every tutor holds a current NSW Working With Children Check and goes through interviews and training before teaching.
