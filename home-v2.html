FAQ

Questions, answered

Here's what business owners usually ask before getting started. Still unsure? Just send me a message.

Ask Me on WhatsApp
How is this different from hiring an agency?

With an agency, you're probably paying for more and communicating through account managers instead of the person actually doing your work. With me, it's direct — you talk to me, I do the work myself, and there's no agency fee added to the cost.

Should I choose Flexible or Full-Time Support?

If you need less than 25 hours a week, or want to start small, Flexible Support is the easier starting point. If you need consistent, ongoing help, Full-Time Support gives you a lower hourly rate and steady coverage.

What if I only need a few hours a month?

That's exactly what Flexible Support is built for — you only pay for the hours you actually need, with no minimum commitment.

Can I hire you for just one project?

Yes — that's exactly what the Custom Project option is for. Websites, CRM setups, automation, or one-off work all fall under this, scoped and quoted to what you actually need.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No — you can pause or stop at any time. There's no long-term contract required.

Is booking a Discovery Call really free?

Yes — no cost, and no obligation to book anything after the call.

Do I need to prepare anything before the Discovery Call?

No — just show up as you are. We'll talk through your business and what you need help with.

What software and tools do you use?

I'm skilled across a range of platforms. Here's what I typically use, by service:

  • Administrative Support: GoHighLevel, Microsoft Office, Google Workspace, Notion, and more.
  • Website Design & Development: WordPress, GoHighLevel, Figma, Wix Studio, and more.
  • CRM & Automation: HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Zapier, Mailchimp, and more.
  • Customer Support: Freshdesk, Zoho Desk, WhatsApp Business, Gmail, and more.
  • Social Media & Design: Canva, Meta Business Suite, Adobe Photoshop, Instagram, and more.
  • Business Operations: Asana, Monday.com, Trello, Google Sheets, and more.

If you already have your own tools in place, that's completely fine too — I'm a fast learner and happy to work within whatever platform you're already using.

Is my business information kept confidential?

Yes. I've handled confidential communications and sensitive records professionally throughout my career, and that same discretion applies to every business I work with.
